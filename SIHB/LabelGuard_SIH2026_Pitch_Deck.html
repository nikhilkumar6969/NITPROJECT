PROBLEM STATEMENT & PROPOSED SOLUTION

Real-World Challenges

  • Slow Manual Audits: Officers physically inspect thousands of packages across physical retail and digital marketplaces.
  • Missing Mandatory Declarations: Improper MRP, missing Unit Sale Price (USP), unlisted Country of Origin, and hidden tax notices go undetected.
  • Unverifiable Font & Contrast: Rule 13 mandates 1.6mm font height and WCAG contrast that cannot be verified accurately by human eye.
  • E-Commerce Blindspots: Digital product pages on Amazon, Flipkart, Blinkit, Zepto lack systematic statutory compliance auditing.

The LabelGuard Solution

  • Deep Learning OCR Engine: FastAPI + RapidOCR (PP-OCRv4 DBNet) microservice extracting spatial text and bounding boxes in <2.3s.
  • 36-Rule Statutory Engine: Automated evaluation of 28 physical label rules + 8 e-commerce listing rules under LMPCR 2011.
  • Font & Luminance Analysis: Spatial pixel sampling calculating font height in mm + WCAG luminance contrast ratios.
  • Human-in-the-Loop & Legal Dossiers: Officer review workspace with override controls and instant PDF/DOCX legal notice generation.
Target Users: Legal Metrology Officers, FSSAI Inspectors, E-Commerce Compliance Teams LabelGuard Platform

Slide 03 / 08

UNIQUE VALUE PROPOSITION & INNOVATION

Legal Safety & Ethical AI Architecture

Strict non-autonomous AI design: LabelGuard provides automated evidentiary data (bounding box coordinates, confidence scores, and rule references), but enforces officer verification before issuing statutory notices.

Dual-Mode Edge & Cloud Deployment

Operates online via Supabase Cloud (PostgreSQL, Auth, Storage) OR completely offline using Local Mock Mode, enabling seamless field inspections in remote retail areas without internet access.

Multi-Channel Physical & Digital Audit

Single unified compliance engine auditing both physical retail packaging photos (camera upload) AND digital product listings across Amazon, Flipkart, Blinkit, Zepto, JioMart, and BigBasket.

Automated Legal Dossier & DOCX Drafting

Generates official tamper-evident PDF inspection certificates AND fully editable Microsoft Word (.docx) formal legal notices under Section 36 of the Legal Metrology Act, 2009.

Slide 05 / 08

TECHNICAL APPROACH & TECH STACK

6-Step Processing Pipeline

  1. 1 Image Capture & Preprocessing: Blur detection, luminance assessment & canvas normalization.
  2. 2 RapidOCR ONNX Engine: Runs PP-OCRv4 DBNet + SVTR with 4-orientation rotation scan.
  3. 3 Regex Normalization: Normalizes currency (₹), dates, SI units, and FSSAI numbers.
  4. 4 36-Rule Compliance Engine: Checks mandatory declarations, font height (mm) & contrast.
  5. 5 Officer Determination Workspace: Human review card supporting finding-level overrides.
  6. 6 Dossier Export: Generates immutable log + official PDF certificate + editable DOCX notice.
